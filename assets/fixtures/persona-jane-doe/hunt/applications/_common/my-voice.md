## Patterns Hope learned

<!-- Newest first. One entry per pattern. hope-write appends after each edit. -->

### 2 Oct 2026: A thank-you gets one plain line about the call. No recap.
- **Hope wrote:** "Thank you so much for taking the time to speak with me today. I really enjoyed learning about the team, the token editor and your interview process, and I'm excited about the opportunity to bring my design-systems experience to Acme."
- **Jane changed it to:** "Thanks for the time today. I liked hearing what your customers struggle with."
- **The rule from now on:** One line about the call, in plain words, then stop. No recap of what was discussed, no "excited about the opportunity", no selling.

## Banned words and shapes

- No em dashes.
- "excited" (as in "excited about the opportunity"). Jane cut it on 2 Oct 2026.
- "passionate". Jane: "Everyone is passionate. Say what I did."
