## The role

Acme Design Systems makes the tools product teams use to build and keep a design system: a token editor, a component catalog, and docs that stay in sync with code.
We are about 120 people. Our customers are design and engineering teams at software companies.

As a Senior Product Designer, you will own the design of the token editor and the component catalog.
You will work every day with the engineers who build them, and with the customers who use them.

What you will do:

- Design new features for the token editor, from first sketch to shipped release.
- Run research sessions with design-system teams at customer companies.
- Prototype interactions and test them with real users.
- Write short design proposals and review them with engineering.
- Help shape Acme's own design language.

Travel: up to 10%, mostly customer visits.

## Must-haves

- 7+ years of product design, at least 3 in B2B software.
- Shipped work on a design system or a developer tool.
- Strong interaction design and prototyping.
- Works directly with engineers, day to day.
- A portfolio that shows process, not only final screens.

## Nice-to-haves

- Experience with design tokens and theming.
- Writing: design proposals, RFCs, docs.
- Deep typography.
- Can read and write basic HTML, CSS or React.
- Has mentored other designers.
